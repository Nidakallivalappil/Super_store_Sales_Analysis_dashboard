# Super Store Sales Analysis Dashboard 📊

## 📌 Project Overview
The **Super Store Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive data visualization project developed using Tableau. It analyzes sales performance, regional trends, product performance, logistics operations, and customer behavior to generate meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Monitor overall sales and profit performance.
- Analyze sales across different regions.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Evaluate shipping and logistics performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Support strategic business decisions through interactive visualizations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Tableau** – Interactive dashboards and data visualization.
- **Microsoft Excel / CSV** – Data source and data preparation.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Executive Summary
Provides an overview of overall business performance through key performance indicators (KPIs) and sales trends.

- Overall sales and profit performance.
- Key business performance indicators.
- Sales and profit trends.
- High-level business performance overview.

### 2. Regional Analysis
Analyzes business performance across different geographical regions.

- Compare sales and profit across regions.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing regions.
- Explore geographical sales distribution.
- Understand regional business performance.

### 3. Product Analysis
Evaluates product performance to identify profitable products and categories.

- Analyze sales by product category and sub-category.
- Identify top-performing products.
- Compare product sales and profitability.
- Identify products that may require improvement.

### 4. Logistics Analysis
Examines shipping operations and delivery-related performance.

- Analyze sales by shipping mode.
- Compare shipping methods.
- Explore shipping-related performance patterns.
- Support logistics and operational decision-making.

### 5. Customer Analysis
Provides insights into customer segments and purchasing behavior.

- Analyze sales by customer segment.
- Identify valuable customer groups.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Compare customer contributions to sales and profit.

## 🔍 Business Value
This dashboard helps users explore business performance from five perspectives: executive reporting, regional performance, product profitability, logistics operations, and customer behavior. These insights can support better sales strategies, operational improvements, and business planning.
